How it works

1

Point it at a video

Paste a YouTube link or upload your own file. Indexing runs once, in the background, nothing to configure.

2

It watches and listens

Frames are read and speech is transcribed, then aligned on one timeline, so a slide and the sentence explaining it stay connected.

3

Ask, then watch

Every answer cites the moments it came from. Click one and the video plays from that exact second, so you can check the claim yourself.
